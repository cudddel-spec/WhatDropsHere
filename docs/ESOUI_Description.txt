[size=5][b]What Drops Here?[/b] [color=#FF8800](Beta)[/color][/size]

[b]Your own loot table for every source in Tamriel – right next to your crosshair.[/b]

What Drops Here? remembers what every loot source has given you so far: resource nodes, chests and containers, corpses, critters, pickpocketing and theft. Aim at a source and you see its statistics before you loot it.

ESO has no fixed loot tables like other games: almost every source can drop almost anything, and a lot depends on zone and level. So instead of guessing, What Drops Here? builds [b]your personal statistics[/b], source by source, as you play.

[i]Example: after looting a few Alit, you can see at a glance that leather drops in about 80 % of them – handy when you plan where to farm materials.[/i]

[color=#FFAA00][b]Languages:[/b] English and German. Other client languages show English texts; tracking works with every client language.[/color]

[size=4][b]What is tracked[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Resource nodes[/b] – main material and bonus finds (furnishing materials, style materials, tempers …)
[*][b]Chests and containers[/b] – chests, sacks, barrels, crates, backpacks
[*][b]Corpses[/b] of enemies, animals and NPCs
[*][b]Critters[/b] you pick up with "Take" (insects, jellyfish, moths …)
[*][b]Pickpocketing[/b] and [b]theft[/b] from owned containers – stored separately
[*][b]Leads[/b] (Antiquities)
[/list]
Every find is stored with its item ID and category, per source and zone, account-wide for all your characters.

[size=4][b]Crosshair display[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]How often you looted the source, average gold, and for each entry the amount and the share of loots it appeared in
[*]Also works for [b]living[/b] enemies, animals and NPCs (outside of combat)
[*]Hidden in combat
[*]Filters by item quality and by source type: enemies, chests and containers, resources, critters and other, theft
[*][b]All settings only affect the display[/b] – everything is always recorded, and changes apply to past data as well
[/list]

[size=4][b]Commands[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]/wdh_menu[/b] – settings (requires LibAddonMenu-2.0)
[*][b]/wdh[/b] – statistics of the last looted source, [b]/wdh <name>[/b] – search a source (current zone first, then all zones)
[*][b]/wdh_zone[/b], [b]/wdh_zones[/b] – sources in the current zone, zones with data
[*][b]/wdh_hud[/b], [b]/wdh_units[/b], [b]/wdh_show 0-5[/b], [b]/wdh_names 0-5[/b] – display options
[*][b]/wdh_debug[/b] – debug output in chat, [b]/wdh_reset yes[/b] – delete all data
[/list]

[size=4][b]Known limitations[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]AoE looting:[/b] If the game combines several corpses into one loot window, it only reports one name. Such loots are stored separately as "group loot" and do not distort the statistics of single sources. For exact numbers per enemy, turn off AoE looting in the game settings.
[*][b]Not every enemy[/b] is recognized by the game under the crosshair; for those, the display stays empty.
[*][b]Sources with the same name[/b] in one zone are combined – that is intended, it is how the statistics are built.
[*][b]Sources are stored by name in your client language[/b], so data from different client languages does not mix.
[*][b]Normal and veteran[/b] are not yet distinguished in dungeons and trials.
[*]Data is saved on logout, /reloadui and a normal exit – not if the game crashes.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Tested with[/b][/size]
Much Smarter AutoLoot and Smarter AutoLoot. The addon records what a source [i]offered[/i], not just what you picked up – so the filters of your auto-loot addon do not distort the statistics.
